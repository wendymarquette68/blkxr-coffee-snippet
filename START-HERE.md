# Add the BLKXR Coffee Maker to Your World

You can do this on the Altspace website. You do not need Unity.

## Add your coffee maker

1. Sign in to **altvr.app** and open the world you own.
2. Open **Edit world**, then click **Snippets**. A snippet is a ready-made item you can add to a world.
3. Click **All** and search for **BLKXR Coffee Maker**.
4. Click the coffee maker's picture.
5. Click **Copy code & open index.html**. This copies the coffee maker's instructions and opens your world's page editor.
6. Look for **</body>** near the bottom of the page. Click just before it, start a new line, and press **Ctrl + V** to paste. Keep everything that was already on the page.
7. Save the file. Leave your world and enter it again to load the coffee maker.

**Use the existing BLKXR Coffee Maker. You do not need to click New snippet.**

## Put it where you want it

Yes, you can choose where it sits. The website uses numbers to place it.

In the part you pasted, find **position**. It has three numbers:

- The first moves it in one direction across the floor.
- The middle number raises or lowers it.
- The last moves it in the other direction across the floor.

For example, **position="0 1 0"** puts the bottom of the machine one metre above the floor level at the world's centre. Your countertop may need different numbers.

Change one number at a time, save, and re-enter your world to check it. Use small changes such as **0.1** to move it about four inches.

To turn the machine, find **rotation** and change its middle number. **rotation="0 90 0"** turns it a quarter turn. Keep **scale** at **1** to keep its normal size.

Leave the long website address in the pasted part alone. That address loads the coffee maker.

## Make a coffee

1. Click the large round **GRIND** control. You will hear the grinder.
2. Click the gold **BREW** button. It turns green while the coffee is being made.
3. Wait about **12 seconds**.
4. Pick up the cup by its **handle**.
5. Put the cup back upright on its saucer when you want to make another coffee.

**GRIND and BREW are the working controls.** The DOSE and DOUBLE labels are decorative.

## If you get stuck

- **I cannot find it in the list:** Make sure you selected **All**, not Mine.
- **I do not see it in my world:** Save your changes, leave and re-enter, then check the position numbers. It may be at the world's centre or below your countertop.
- **BREW will not start:** Wait for grinding or brewing to finish, and check that the cup is upright on its saucer.
- **I cannot pick up the cup:** Wait until brewing finishes and grab the handle.
- **I cannot find the page or placement settings:** Ask Wendy for help before changing the rest of your world's page.
